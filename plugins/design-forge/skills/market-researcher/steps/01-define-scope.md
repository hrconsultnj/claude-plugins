# Step 1: Define Research Scope

Clarify what we're researching before executing any searches.

## Questions to Ask

```yaml
questions_to_ask:
  - Which competitors are in scope? (name 3-5 specifically; "all competitors" is too broad)
  - What feature set or product dimension are we comparing?
  - What market segment / customer profile?
  - What's the business question this research will answer?
  - What's the decision timeline? (informs depth — same-day decision vs. quarterly planning)
  - Are there competitors we're explicitly NOT studying? (and why)
```

## Classify Research Type

Based on the user's request, determine which research type applies:

| Type | Trigger Phrases | Depth |
|------|----------------|-------|
| Competitor Deep-Dive | "Tell me everything about competitor X", "How does X work?" | 1-2 hours |
| Feature Matrix Audit | "Which competitors ship feature Y?", "Compare X, Y, Z on features" | 1-2 hours |
| Pricing Intelligence | "How does X price?", "Pricing landscape for [category]" | 30-60 min |
| Positioning Study | "How is X positioned vs Y?", "What's our positioning vs competitors?" | 1-2 hours |
| Market Gap Hunt | "What are competitors missing?", "What do users complain about?" | 1-2 hours |

## Determine Research Scope

From the user's request, extract:

1. **Competitor List** — 3-5 named companies/products (force specificity; reject "all competitors")
2. **Feature/Dimension Set** — what aspects are under comparison (features, pricing tiers, target segment, tech stack, etc.)
3. **Market Segment** — the customer profile we care about (solo founders, mid-market SaaS, enterprise, regulated industries, etc.)
4. **Business Question** — what decision will this research inform (build feature X? reprice tier Y? reposition product Z?)
5. **Constraints** — public-info-only vs. paywalled-OK vs. interview-required; freshness target (last 6 months? all-time?)

If the user's request is ambiguous, ask for clarification on the top 2-3 unknowns. Do not ask all six questions — infer what you can from context.

**Hard rule:** never proceed past Step 1 with a vague competitor list. "X, Y, and similar" is not a competitor list. Force named competitors.
